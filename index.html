How ULTRA stays reliable.

Privacy-first does not mean fragile. ULTRA uses Android-native systems to keep reminders dependable.

1. Pick a trigger

Create a reminder manually, from a template, or through Smart Input. Choose time, place, activity, device, countdown, or routine logic.

2. ULTRA monitors locally

Your reminders and settings are stored on-device. Android services handle geofences, activity recognition, alarms, and background checks.

3. The alarm breaks through

Get full-screen alarms, custom sounds, vibration, spoken descriptions, smart snooze, or challenge dismissal when a reminder really matters.
